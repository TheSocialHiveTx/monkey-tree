OUR COMPREHENSIVE ARBORIST SERVICES

Every job is supervised by an ISA Certified Arborist with heavy rigging equipment, sharp STIHL chainsaws, and thorough cleanup. Choose your service below to get an upfront quote.

FIELD WORK-ORDER SERVICE MENU HOUSTON • HARRIS • MONTGOMERY • FORT BEND
  • ✔

    TREE REMOVAL

    Controlled section-by-section lowering of trees of any height or diameter. We dismantle dead, dying, or crowding trees in tight residential quarters without disturbing surrounding structures or lawns.

  • ✔

    TREE TRIMMING & PRUNING

    Arborist-directed canopy elevation, deadwood thinning, and structural weight-reduction pruning. We follow strict ISA tree care guidelines to promote long-term vitality, storm resilience, and sunlight penetration.

  • ✔

    STUMP GRINDING

    Eliminate unsightly trip hazards, termite harbors, and lawnmower blockers. We grind stumps 6 to 12 inches below grade, chase surface runner roots, and leave the mulch level for fresh sod or planting.

  • ✔

    STORM DAMAGE CLEANUP

    Houston hurricanes and severe convective thunderstorms drop heavy live oak and pine limbs fast. Our rapid crew cuts, safely rigs off roofs or fences, and hauls every scrap of debris away.

  • ✔

    HAZARDOUS TREE REMOVAL

    Specialized extraction of severely compromised trees: hollowed trunks, split crotches, trees leaning over power drops, and root plate failures. Zero room for error — tackled with professional arboricultural rigging.

  • ✔

    PLANT HEALTHCARE

    Preserve your prized shade trees. Deep root feeding, Houston gumbo soil decompaction, fungal treatments, pest deterrents, and tree vigor revitalization programs developed for Gulf Coast microclimates.

  • ✔

    TREE RISK ASSESSMENTS

    Comprehensive visual inspections and sounding tests by ISA Certified Arborist Corey Coupland. Identify hidden internal decay, root severance, structural defects, and receive actionable preservation or removal plans.
